LED Basics — Elegoo Uno R3 Starter Kit

Complete

First lesson from the Elegoo Uno R3 Basic Starter Kit: wiring an LED to a breadboard and observing how its brightness changes with different resistor values. Swapping in a 220 ohm, 1k ohm, and 10k ohm resistor showed a direct trade-off between current-limiting and brightness: higher resistance means a dimmer LED. This was my first hands-on look at how a resistor protects an LED from drawing too much current and burning out, and my first time reading breadboard rows and resistor color codes in a real build rather than just a diagram.
